# Care Circle

A shared, source-cited family brain for caring for a parent. Built from scratch during the Own Your Intelligence Hackathon on September 27, 2026.

Care Circle turns synthetic visit notes into connected records in GBrain, preserves conflicting medication claims, and prepares a brief for the next specialist visit. It organizes information and cites sources. It never recommends doses or treatments.

All people, providers, records, calls, and websites in the demo are synthetic. Not medical advice.

Implementation is in progress. See [the contract](docs/CONTRACT.md), [the plan](docs/PLAN.md), and [current status](docs/STATUS.md). Sponsor integrations will be labeled according to what actually runs. No trained-model or external-integration results are claimed yet.
